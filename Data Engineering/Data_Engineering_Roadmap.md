## ✅ Available Now

| Step | Folder | What it covers | Why it comes at this point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | [`01-fundamentals/`](./01-fundamentals/DE_Fundamentals.md) | What data engineering is, the DE lifecycle, roles & titles, ETL, types of data, storage systems, the Uber case study | The big picture — what the job actually is and why it exists, before touching any tool |
| **2** | [`02-data-modeling-and-databases/`](./02-data-modeling-and-databases/Data_Modeling_Databases.md) | Data models, databases, primary/foreign keys, relationships, fact & dimension tables | You can't write good SQL until you understand what a table, a key, and a relationship actually *are* |
| **3** | [`03-sql/`](./03-sql/SQL_Reference.md) | RDBMS, database design steps, full SQL syntax (CRUD, filtering, ordering, aggregates, joins, subqueries, window functions), Python + MySQL | SQL is the hands-on skill that puts steps 1 & 2 into practice — this is where you actually query data |

**Why this order?** Concepts → structure → syntax. Learning SQL syntax before understanding *why* tables are shaped the way they are (step 2) makes the syntax feel arbitrary. Understanding the DE lifecycle first (step 1) gives every later tool a "why," not just a "how."
